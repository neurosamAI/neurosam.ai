## What Each Option Brings

### Touch Kiosk
A one-way, click-through menu interface. Efficient for standardized tasks like ordering or ticketing, but **accessibility for older adults, foreign visitors, and the visually impaired is weak**, and adding a new task means redeveloping the screen. As of January 28, 2026, existing kiosks are also subject to the Korea Disability Discrimination Act's accessibility requirements, creating the added burden of bolting on voice and accessibility features separately.

### Chatbot SaaS
Text-based reception on the web and in apps. Easy and inexpensive to deploy, but **it can't serve physical sites** and offers no multimodal (face/voice) interaction. A good fit for online customer-support assistance, but not for physical spaces like stores, lobbies, or service desks.

### Industrial Guide Robot
Capable of multimodal interaction, but **the cost to deploy is very high** (tens of millions to hundreds of millions of won per unit), and because the software is tied to the hardware, domain customization and updates are slow. Suitable for select large-scale facilities.

### neurosam.AI Agent-R — AI Reception Agent
A **domain persona platform** that integrates face recognition (Vision AI) + voice conversation (STT/TTS) + an LLM/RAG persona. Key differentiators:

- **Voice, multilingual support, and accessibility met by design** → automatic compliance with the Korea Disability Discrimination Act
- **Integrated 24/7 AI phone reception** → in-person reception plus after-hours calls in a single solution
- **Just swap the per-site system prompt and RAG knowledge base** → rapid deployment to new domains
- **Government-recognized AI supplier (MSIT · NIPA, 2026)** → an independently vetted vendor

## When Is Agent-R the Right Fit?

- When you need reception in a **physical space that welcomes people** — offices, hotels, stores, hospitals, public institutions
- When **staffing for night, holiday, or multilingual reception is hard to secure**
- When you need to meet the **mandated kiosk accessibility requirements under the Korea Disability Discrimination Act**
- When an industrial robot is too much but a simple kiosk isn't enough
