Agent-R Platform

A 24/7 unmanned reception AI kiosk agent robot
— it runs as a domain persona

Agent-R is a 24/7 unmanned reception solution that unifies on-premise service robots and AI callbots on a single platform. Swap only the site-specific system prompt and RAG knowledge base, and it instantly runs as a new domain persona — so AI covers every customer touchpoint, from greeting visitors to handling missed calls.

AI facial recognition & vision perception

Even when several people approach at once, it instantly recognizes each face and greets returning visitors by name with a personalized welcome. When someone loses interest and turns away, it naturally wraps up the conversation — reception with autonomous judgment.

Multi-Face Detection Returning-visitor recognition Autonomous engagement

Seamless voice conversation

Even in the brief moment while the AI prepares a response, it keeps the conversation flowing with natural filler phrases. The instant a user starts speaking, it switches to listening mode — so the exchange feels just like talking with a person.

Domain Persona Engine

A different persona for every site,
the same platform.

Conventional kiosks and chatbots had to be rebuilt from scratch for every new site. Agent-R decouples the system prompt and RAG knowledge base into modules, so you only define the site-specific persona and the same platform deploys quickly to a new site. This makes the SaaS economics of building once and scaling to many sites genuinely possible.

Modular personas

The system prompt defines personality, tone, and role, while the RAG knowledge base injects domain knowledge separately.

Fast new-domain deployment

When adopting a new site, swapping the persona alone lets you apply to a new domain in a short timeframe.

Persona library

Domain personas across healthcare, hotels, public sector, retail, and more accumulate and expand into a marketplace.

Customer data isolation

Per-site RAG index separation provides data isolation and access control as a default design.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an AI reception agent and a regular kiosk?
A regular kiosk is a static, touchscreen-based interface. Agent-R, by contrast, proactively perceives visitors through facial recognition, responds with natural-language voice conversation, and judges interest to autonomously start or end a conversation. It delivers intelligent unmanned guidance that sees, listens, and reasons like a person.
Can I adopt Agent-R using the AI Voucher?
Yes. neurosam.AI has been selected for both the 2026 Ministry of Science and ICT AI Voucher Supplier POOL and the AI Youth-Startup Voucher supplier program. Depending on your circumstances, you can choose either voucher to cover the cost of adopting Agent-R — so you can begin your AI transformation without an upfront cost burden.
How does 24/7 AI phone answering work?
The AI phone assistant answers calls placed to your company's main line on your behalf. It responds accurately using a customer-specific knowledge base (RAG), and after each call it automatically generates a conversation summary and follow-up actions and delivers them to the right team member. It runs without interruption at night, on holidays, and whenever staff are away.
Which industries can use Agent-R?
It can be used in every space that welcomes people — smart offices (AI visitor management, staff paging), hotels & resorts (24/7 unmanned concierge, unmanned check-in), retail & F&B (in-store guidance, voice ordering), and healthcare institutions (unmanned intake, wayfinding to exam rooms).
What level is the facial-recognition-based visitor management?
Agent-R's Vision AI detects and tracks each face in real time even when several people approach simultaneously, and it recognizes returning visitors to greet them by name with a personalized welcome. It enables AI facial recognition access control that operates reliably across varied lighting conditions and multi-directional approaches.
How long does deployment take?
Agent-R's key advantage is its domain persona engine. Swap only the site-specific system prompt and RAG knowledge base, and the same platform runs as a new site persona — so persona deployment is possible within a week for a standard adoption scenario. The full timeline, including hardware installation and system integration, depends on your environment and is shared during the consultation stage.
How much is the self-payment when using the AI Voucher?
The demand company's self-payment ratio is determined each year by the government matching rate set by NIPA. Typically the government covers a set portion and the demand company self-pays the rest, with the exact ratio and eligibility differing each year by the program announcement. Because neurosam.AI is registered in both the AI Voucher Supplier POOL and the AI Youth-Startup Shared-Growth Voucher Supplier POOL, we help match the right voucher to your situation during the consultation stage.
Can we see a demo or run a PoC?
Yes. During your evaluation we can provide a product demo, send technical materials, and discuss a PoC tailored to your environment. Reach out through the consultation form and we'll reply within 24 hours. Feel free to inquire even if you only want to review information, with no obligation to adopt.
